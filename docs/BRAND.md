# MediCrew — official brand baseline

## Approved logo

The approved identity is the rounded **M** symbol with its centered medical cross, paired with the **MediCrew** wordmark and the tagline **CARE MOVES FORWARD**. Do not redraw, rotate, outline or separate the cross from the symbol.

## Official files

- `assets/icon.svg`: application icon and standalone brand mark on the official mint background.
- `assets/medicrew-logo.svg`: horizontal logo lockup for the website, stores and documents.
- `assets/notification-icon.svg`: monochrome notification mark.

## Core colors

- Ink: `#0D1513`
- MediCrew dark green: `#08785C`
- MediCrew green: `#19B985`
- Gradient middle: `#34CC94`
- Gradient highlight: `#69DFA4`
- Brand mint background: `#ECF8F3`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Transport and event colors are supporting product-navigation colors. They must not replace the green MediCrew master identity in the logo.

## Usage

- Use the complete horizontal lockup on the presentation website and store graphics.
- Use the standalone mark for the app icon, favicon, avatar and compact mobile headers.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the width of the medical cross.
- On photography or busy backgrounds, place the logo on a solid white or brand-mint panel.
- Never use the previous black-background icon or the version with the cross placed on the right side.
